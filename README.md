
Artificial intelligence is a modern tool that allows us to automate all processes, thereby increasing productivity.


The ability to have this tool on our computers is currently very limited, as it requires the assistance of large companies that provide their services, such as through the use of APIs.


The artificial intelligence in this project is based on the use of other APIs from various artificial intelligence systems. With this, our system can behave like a real one, but it is subject to changes made by these companies.


Due to changes in the terms and conditions for using APIs, many of the functions in this project may stop working depending on when these companies decide to continue making this technology available to the public.


To access the system, you need an account; depending on each company’s requirements, they may request a monetary exchange. This is entirely subject to their laws and has nothing to do with the creator of this project or any individual. Access is at your own risk.
